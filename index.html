Who is Moto Desk built for?

Independent dealerships and growing dealership groups in South Africa. From dealer principals and branch managers to salespeople and admin teams, Moto Desk is built around the people running the business.

What can I explore in early access?

The current platform centres on the Lead Centre, an owner Command Centre, staff management and the first phase of payroll. Stock, deals, websites and broader finance workflows are part of the roadmap.

Are all the integrations available today?

The keyboard shows the ecosystem we’re building around: DiskDrive, Meta, WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook, Excel, Claude and OpenAI. Connections are being introduced in phases. Availability and setup requirements will be confirmed during onboarding.
